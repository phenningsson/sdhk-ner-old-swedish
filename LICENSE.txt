DATA SOURCES AND ATTRIBUTIONS
================================================================================

This repository includes data derived from the following sources. Users who
redistribute or build on the derived datasets should additionally respect the
licensing terms of each upstream source.

1. SDHK — Svenskt Diplomatariums Huvudkartotek
   The Main Catalogue of the Swedish Diplomatarium, distributed by the
   Swedish National Archives (Riksarkivet). Scraped charter JSONs in data/raw/ and all derived CoNLL datasets ultimately originate from SDHK
   Edition (Old Swedish charter text) and Summary (modern Swedish regest)
   fields.
   Source: https://sok.riksarkivet.se/sdhk
   CSV export: https://filer.riksarkivet.se/registerdata/SDHK/csv/sdhk_2411.csv
   License: see the SDHK terms of use at Riksarkivet, CC-BY 4.0 License, https://sok.riksarkivet.se/data-api/nedladdningsbara-datamangder/sdhk-de-svenska-medeltidsbreven.

   Citation:
   @misc{sdhk,
     author = {Riksarkivet},
     title  = {Svenskt Diplomatariums Huvudkartotek (SDHK)},
     url    = {https://sok.riksarkivet.se/sdhk}
   }

2. TORA — Topografiskt Register
   Historical settlement gazetteer maintained by Riksarkivet; used as
   Signal 2 (gazetteer lookup) for Location entities.
   Source: https://riksarkivet.se/tora
   License: see TORA terms of use at Riksarkivet, CC-BY 4.0 License.

   Citation:
   @misc{tora,
     author = {Riksarkivet},
     title  = {TORA — Topografiskt Register},
     url    = {https://riksarkivet.se/tora}
   }

3. DF — Diplomatarium Fennicum
   Finnish medieval charter database, maintained by Kansallisarkisto
   (National Archives of Finland); used to augment TORA with place-name
   coverage for the eastern part of the medieval Swedish realm.
   Source: http://df.narc.fi
   License: see Diplomatarium Fennicum terms of use, CC0 1.0 Universal License.

   Citation:
   @misc{df,
     author = {Kansallisarkisto},
     title  = {Diplomatarium Fennicum},
     url    = {http://df.narc.fi}
   }

4. SMP — Sveriges medeltida personnamn
   Dictionary of Swedish medieval personal names, edited by Institutet för
   språk och folkminnen (Isof); used as Signal 2 (gazetteer lookup) for
   Person entities.
   Source: https://smp.isof.se/
   License: see SMP terms of use at Isof, CC-BY 4.0 License.

   Citation:
   @misc{smp,
     author = {Institutet för språk och folkminnen},
     title  = {Sveriges medeltida personnamn (SMP)},
     url    = {https://smp.isof.se/}
   }

5. KB-BERT (Swedish BERT models from KBLab)
   KBLab/bert-base-swedish-cased and KBLab/bert-base-swedish-cased-ner are
   used as the Signal 1 NER backbone (on modern Swedish summaries) and as
   baseline fine-tuning targets in the NER training experiments.
   Source: https://huggingface.co/KBLab/bert-base-swedish-cased
   License: see the model card on HuggingFace, CC0 1.0 Universal License.

   Citation:
   @misc{malmsten2020playing,
     title={Playing with Words at the National Library of Sweden -- Making a Swedish BERT},
     author={Martin Malmsten and Love Börjeson and Chris Haffenden},
     year={2020},
     eprint={2007.01658},
     archivePrefix={arXiv},
     primaryClass={cs.CL}
    }


6. XLM-RoBERTa
   xlm-roberta-large is used as the base model for MLM domain adaptation
   on Old Swedish; the resulting checkpoint is then fine-tuned for NER.
   Source: https://huggingface.co/FacebookAI/xlm-roberta-large
   License: MIT

   Citation:
   @inproceedings{conneau-etal-2020-unsupervised,
     title     = "Unsupervised Cross-lingual Representation Learning at Scale",
     author    = "Conneau, Alexis and Khandelwal, Kartikay and
                  Goyal, Naman and Chaudhary, Vishrav and Wenzek, Guillaume
                  and Guzm\'an, Francisco and Grave, Edouard and Ott, Myle
                  and Zettlemoyer, Luke and Stoyanov, Veselin",
     booktitle = "Proceedings of the 58th Annual Meeting of the Association
                  for Computational Linguistics",
     year      = "2020",
     url       = "https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.747"
   }
